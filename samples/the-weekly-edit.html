The weekly edit

A few good things from a week of making, reading, and paying attention.

Start with something small

A good website is a collection of small decisions made with care. A heading that says what it means. A paragraph that gets to the point. A little room to breathe.

The details are not the decoration. They are the experience.

Three things worth your time

  1. Revisit an old idea. A little distance can show you what was missing.
  2. Share something useful. The note in your drafts might be just what someone needs.
  3. Leave a little space. Good work needs time away from the screen, too.

Until next week

Keep making things that feel like you. We’ll be here when you’re ready to share them.
